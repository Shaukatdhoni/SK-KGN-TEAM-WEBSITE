SK KGN TEAM ("we", "us", or "our") operates the website skkgnteam.in. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored, disclosed and protected when you visit our website or use its services.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on the service you use and the information you choose to submit, we may collect the following categories of information:

  • Contact information: Name, mobile number, email address and other contact details.
  • Advocate registration information: Name, state, district, professional details, areas of practice, qualifications and other information submitted in an advocate application.
  • Complaint information: Details of a complaint, relevant documents, descriptions of an issue and contact information supplied through a complaint form.
  • Career application information: Details provided by applicants, which may include qualifications, experience, contact details and other application-related information.
  • Technical information: Certain browser, device or usage information may be processed where it is collected through our hosting provider or other technologies used by the website.

Please provide only information that is relevant to your request. Avoid submitting passwords, financial credentials or unnecessary sensitive personal information through ordinary website forms.

3. How We Use Your Information

Information may be used for legitimate and disclosed purposes, including:

  • Processing advocate registration applications.
  • Reviewing applications and managing approval status.
  • Displaying approved advocate profiles in public search results.
  • Receiving, reviewing and responding to complaints.
  • Processing career applications and enquiries.
  • Communicating with users about their submissions or requests.

We will not treat the submission of personal information as unlimited permission to use it for unrelated purposes.

4. Advocate Registration and Public Profiles

SK KGN TEAM may allow advocates to submit professional information for review and possible publication on the advocate search page. The information displayed depends on the details submitted, the approval process and the features of the website.

Approved profiles may display information such as an advocate's name, state, district, professional details and practice areas. Information not intended for public display should not be exposed through public search results or unauthorized database access.

Where a mobile number is masked on the public website, only the intended masked version should be displayed in public search results. Masking a number visually does not, by itself, secure the original number stored in the database.

Users should understand that displaying a profile does not automatically guarantee the completeness, current accuracy or independent verification of every detail in that profile. Verification claims will be made only to the extent supported by the actual checks performed.

7. OTP Verification and Mobile Number Security

SK KGN TEAM may use One-Time Password (OTP) verification to verify control of a mobile number, confirm user requests, reduce unauthorized submissions and protect website services.

Purpose of OTP: OTPs are intended only for the specific verification or authentication purpose for which they are requested. Verification of a mobile number does not constitute proof of a person's legal identity, age, address or ownership of any submitted information.

OTP Confidentiality: Users must not share their OTP with anyone, including persons claiming to represent SK KGN TEAM. Our team will never ask users to disclose an OTP over a phone call, WhatsApp message or email.

Use and Processing: Where OTP services are enabled, the mobile number and related verification information may be processed through our website infrastructure and third-party SMS or verification service providers, solely for the relevant verification and security purposes.

Storage and Security: OTPs are intended to be temporary verification codes. We will take reasonable steps to prevent unauthorized access to verification information. OTP values should not be retained in readable form longer than necessary for verification.

No Financial Authentication: SK KGN TEAM does not require users to share bank OTPs, UPI PINs, ATM PINs, card PINs, passwords or other financial authentication credentials. Never disclose such credentials to anyone.

Third-Party Services: OTP delivery may depend on external telecommunications, SMS gateway or verification providers. Delivery delays, network failures or service interruptions may occur. Their processing of information is also subject to their applicable terms and privacy practices.

Misuse and Unauthorized Activity: Users are responsible for keeping their devices and OTP messages secure. Please inform us promptly if you suspect unauthorized use of your mobile number or account.

Nothing in this section limits any rights available to users or excludes any responsibility that cannot lawfully be excluded under applicable Indian law.

03 Eligibility for Refund

A refund may be considered in circumstances such as:

  • The payment was successfully deducted but the service order was not created due to a technical error.
  • The customer was charged more than once for the same service.
  • SK KGN TEAM is unable to provide the paid service for reasons attributable to us.
  • A cancellation request is received before work on the service has started.
  • A verified payment or technical issue prevents the purchased service from being delivered.
  • A refund is required under applicable law.

Each request will be reviewed based on the payment record, service status and circumstances of the case.

04 Non-Refundable Situations

A refund may generally not be available in the following situations, subject to applicable consumer protection laws:
