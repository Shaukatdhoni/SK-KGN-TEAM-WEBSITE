Refund Policy

Clear information about payments, cancellations, refund eligibility and the refund process for services offered by SK KGN TEAM.

Last Updated: 27 November 2026
This Refund Policy explains the conditions under which customers may request a refund for services purchased through SK KGN TEAM. Please read this policy carefully before making a payment.

01 About This Policy

SK KGN TEAM is an independent private consultancy providing assistance with complaint preparation, application support, submission assistance, follow-ups and selected citizen services.

We are not a government department, government authority or statutory body. Our services are limited to the assistance expressly described under the selected service plan.

02 Payment Policy

Customers are required to review the selected service plan, its price and the scope of assistance before completing payment.

  • Payments must be made through the payment methods provided on our website.
  • Customers should enter accurate payment and contact information.
  • A payment confirmation does not mean that a government authority has accepted or approved a complaint or application.
  • Customers should retain their payment receipt or transaction reference.

03 Eligibility for Refund

A refund may be considered in circumstances such as:

  • The payment was successfully deducted but the service order was not created due to a technical error.
  • The customer was charged more than once for the same service.
  • SK KGN TEAM is unable to provide the paid service for reasons attributable to us.
  • A cancellation request is received before work on the service has started.
  • A verified payment or technical issue prevents the purchased service from being delivered.
  • A refund is required under applicable law.

Each request will be reviewed based on the payment record, service status and circumstances of the case.

04 Non-Refundable Situations

A refund may generally not be available in the following situations, subject to applicable consumer protection laws:

  • The agreed service has already been completed and delivered.
  • The customer changes their mind after the service work has started.
  • A government department delays, rejects or does not act upon a complaint or application, where our agreed assistance has been properly delivered.
  • The customer provides incorrect, incomplete or misleading information that affects the service.
  • The customer does not provide necessary documents or responses within a reasonable time.
Important: These conditions do not exclude any refund, remedy or consumer right that cannot legally be excluded under applicable law.

05 Cancellation Before Service Begins

A customer may request cancellation before work on the purchased service has started.

If the cancellation request is approved and no substantial work has been performed, the eligible amount may be refunded after verification.

Once service work has started, the refund amount, if any, will be assessed according to the work already performed and applicable law.

06 Refund After Service Work Has Started

If a customer requests cancellation after work has started, SK KGN TEAM may review the progress of the service, the work already completed and any applicable refund obligations.

A full or partial refund may be considered where appropriate. No automatic refund is guaranteed merely because a customer changes their mind after work has begun.

07 Completed Services

Once the agreed assistance has been completed and delivered, the service will generally be treated as fulfilled.

A completed service does not guarantee a particular decision, action or outcome by any government authority or third party.

Any statutory rights or remedies available to the customer under applicable law remain unaffected.

08 Government Decisions and Outcomes

SK KGN TEAM provides private assistance with preparing complaints, applications and related documentation, as described in the selected service plan.

The decision to register, investigate, accept, reject or take action on a complaint rests with the relevant government department or competent authority.

A delay, rejection or lack of action by an external authority does not automatically mean that the assistance service was not delivered.

09 Failed or Duplicate Payments

If money is deducted from your account but the order is not confirmed, please contact us with the relevant transaction details.

For duplicate payments, the additional payment may be reviewed for refund after verification.

The original transaction and payment status may be checked with the payment service provider before a refund decision is made.

10 How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, contact SK KGN TEAM and provide the following information:

  • Customer's full name.
  • Registered mobile number.
  • Complaint ID or order reference, if available.
  • Payment transaction ID or payment reference.
  • Date and amount of payment.
  • Reason for requesting a refund.
  • Relevant payment receipt or supporting details, if required.

Providing complete information helps us verify the request and process it more efficiently.

11 Refund Review and Approval

All refund requests are subject to verification and review.

SK KGN TEAM may check payment records, service progress, communication history and other relevant information before making a decision.

Customers may be contacted if additional information is required.

12 Refund Processing Time

Once a refund request has been approved, we generally aim to initiate the refund within 5–7 business days.

The actual time taken for the refunded amount to appear in the customer's account may depend on the bank, card issuer, UPI provider or payment service provider.

The 5–7 business day period refers to our general refund initiation target after approval. It is not a guarantee of the date on which the customer's bank will credit the amount.

13 Refund Method

Where possible, approved refunds will be processed through the original payment method or through a method supported by the payment provider.

Customers may be asked to provide additional information for verification where required.

14 Incorrect or Incomplete Information

Customers are responsible for providing accurate and complete information required for the selected service.

If incorrect or incomplete information causes delays or prevents service delivery, the matter may be reviewed before deciding whether a refund is applicable.

This provision does not limit any rights available to customers under applicable law.

15 Fraudulent or Unauthorized Transactions

If you believe a transaction was made without your authorization, contact your bank or payment provider immediately.

You may also inform SK KGN TEAM so that the relevant payment or order records can be reviewed.

We may request reasonable supporting information to investigate a reported transaction.

16 Changes to This Policy

SK KGN TEAM may update or modify this Refund Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our services, payment processes or applicable legal requirements.

The revised policy will be published on this page with an updated revision date.

Customers are advised to review this page periodically.

17 Applicable Laws and Customer Rights

This Refund Policy shall be interpreted in accordance with the applicable laws of India.

Nothing in this policy is intended to remove, restrict or override any consumer right, remedy or protection that is available under applicable Indian law.

If any provision of this policy is found to be inconsistent with applicable law, the relevant legal requirement will prevail.
