ABOUT THE BOOK

A Message Beyond Borders

A book inviting readers to reflect on the connections that bring people together.

More Than History. A Reflection on Humanity.

United India explores the historical and cultural connections that have shaped the Indian subcontinent.

It encourages readers to reflect on shared heritage, human relationships, mutual understanding and peaceful dialogue.

The book presents an opportunity to think about the values that connect people beyond geographical boundaries.

One Shared Heritage

Borders may divide territories, but history, culture and humanity continue to connect people. United India is an invitation to explore these connections and reflect on understanding and dialogue.
