1. Introduction

SK KGN TEAM ("we", "us", or "our") operates the website skkgnteam.in. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored, disclosed and protected when you visit our website or use its services.

Our website provides information about our services, advocate registration and search facilities, complaint-related assistance, career applications and other features made available from time to time.

By using our website or submitting information through its forms, you acknowledge this Privacy Policy. Where consent is required under applicable law, we will seek that consent as appropriate.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on the service you use and the information you choose to submit, we may collect the following categories of information:

  • Contact information: Name, mobile number, email address and other contact details.
  • Advocate registration information: Name, state, district, professional details, areas of practice, qualifications and other information submitted in an advocate application.
  • Complaint information: Details of a complaint, relevant documents, descriptions of an issue and contact information supplied through a complaint form.
  • Career application information: Details provided by applicants, which may include qualifications, experience, contact details and other application-related information.
  • Technical information: Certain browser, device or usage information may be processed where it is collected through our hosting provider or other technologies used by the website.

Please provide only information that is relevant to your request. Avoid submitting passwords, financial credentials or unnecessary sensitive personal information through ordinary website forms.

3. How We Use Your Information

Information may be used for legitimate and disclosed purposes, including:

  • Processing advocate registration applications.
  • Reviewing applications and managing approval status.
  • Displaying approved advocate profiles in public search results.
  • Receiving, reviewing and responding to complaints.
  • Processing career applications and enquiries.
  • Communicating with users about their submissions or requests.
  • Maintaining website functionality and security.
  • Preventing misuse, fraud and unauthorized access.
  • Complying with applicable legal obligations.

We will not treat the submission of personal information as unlimited permission to use it for unrelated purposes.

4. Advocate Registration and Public Profiles

SK KGN TEAM may allow advocates to submit professional information for review and possible publication on the advocate search page. The information displayed depends on the details submitted, the approval process and the features of the website.

Approved profiles may display information such as an advocate's name, state, district, professional details and practice areas. Information not intended for public display should not be exposed through public search results or unauthorized database access.

Where a mobile number is masked on the public website, only the intended masked version should be displayed in public search results. Masking a number visually does not, by itself, secure the original number stored in the database.

Users should understand that displaying a profile does not automatically guarantee the completeness, current accuracy or independent verification of every detail in that profile. Verification claims will be made only to the extent supported by the actual checks performed.

Information submitted through complaint, career or other application forms may contain personal or confidential details. We use such information to process the relevant submission, communicate with the submitter and perform related administrative or service functions.

Users should avoid including unnecessary personal details about themselves or other individuals. Before uploading documents, please ensure that you are authorized to provide them.

Access to complaint and application records should be restricted to authorized persons and systems that require the information for legitimate operational purposes.
